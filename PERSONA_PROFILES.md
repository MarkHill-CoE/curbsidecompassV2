# Curbside Compass — Persona Profiles & Scoring Ranges

This document details the **16 Edmonton Curbside Policy Personas**, including their complete **descriptions**, exact **X and Y score value ranges**, classification thresholds, policy priorities, and municipal alignments.

---

## 1. Scoring System Overview

Every answer choice in Questions 1 through 8 awards positive, negative, or neutral points along two distinct axes:

- **Horizontal Axis ($X$): Access & Funding Structure**
  - **Negative ($-17$ to $-1$):** City-wide / Taxpayer funding & Open access
  - **Positive ($0$ to $+17$):** Targeted / User-fee funding & Regulated access
  - **Total Range:** $-17 \le X \le +17$

- **Vertical Axis ($Y$): Regulatory Enforcement & Rule Intensity**
  - **Negative ($-18$ to $-1$):** Strict, structured, and defined rules (Restrictive)
  - **Positive ($0$ to $+18$):** Minimal rules, open parking, and flexible courtesy (Open)
  - **Total Range:** $-18 \le Y \le +18$
